Current product

APEX turns a difficult-conversation practice session into structured feedback

  1. Select a case
  2. Conduct clinician–patient conversation
  3. Store ordered transcript
  4. Evaluate communication
  5. Generate feedback + metrics
  6. Learner / admin review

Trainee perspective

Choose a training case, communicate with a virtual patient, then review strengths, missed empathic opportunities, SPIKES coverage, and suggested improvements.

Administrator / researcher perspective

Manage cases and plugin selections, inspect frozen session provenance, review aggregate analytics, and produce controlled research exports.

Repository basis: README.md, session/dialogue/scoring services, and frontend/src/pages/Admin.tsx. The comparison UI shown later is a meeting mockup, not the current production frontend.

Walkthrough case

One conversation contains both effective communication and repairable misses

Synthetic demonstration transcript — not clinical data. Entirely fictional; no person, medical record, private transcript, or confidential source example is represented.
1
Clinician

Thank you for meeting with me. Before we discuss the biopsy, could you tell me what you understand about the tests so far?

2
Patient

I know the scan found something unusual. I am scared it might be cancer, and I keep thinking about what that would mean for my daughter.

3
Clinician

The biopsy shows lymphoma. It is a malignancy involving cells in the lymphatic system, and we will need immunophenotyping before choosing treatment.

4
Patient

Malignancy means cancer, right? I feel like we moved past how frightening this is.

5
Clinician

You are right, and I am sorry I moved too quickly. This is frightening news. Would it be okay if I pause and then explain what lymphoma means in plain language?

6
Patient

Yes, please. I need to know whether it can be treated.

7
Clinician

Lymphoma is a cancer that starts in part of the immune system. There are different types, and many can be treated. The next test identifies the type. Does that explanation make sense, and what are you taking from it?

8
Patient

I understand that it is cancer and that the next test tells us the type. I am still scared about caring for my daughter and missing work.

9
Clinician

I hear that your daughter and work are central concerns. Before we make a plan, what matters most to you over the next few weeks?

10
Patient

I want to stay well enough to care for her, and I would like my sister with me for the specialist visit.

11
Clinician

Let me summarize: the biopsy confirms lymphoma, the next test identifies the type, and then the specialist will discuss treatment. We will arrange that visit, include your sister, and give you the terms in writing. What questions would you like us to address first?

12
Patient

Can we discuss the likely timeline and who I should call if the worry becomes overwhelming?

Original APEX foundations

SPIKES structures the conversation; AFCE locates empathic opportunities and responses

Conversation structure

SPIKES

Tracks the structure of breaking-bad-news communication and supports stage-aware patient behavior and learner feedback.

SettingPerceptionInvitationKnowledgeEmotionStrategy / Summary

Empathy discourse

AFCE

Represents emotional and appraisal spans, elicitations, empathic responses, missed opportunities, and relevant discourse-level span/relation concepts.

FeelingJudgmentAppreciationElicitationResponseMissed opportunity

Experimental complement

ACE-CT-inspired

Explores broader communication-quality dimensions in the same transcript. It does not replace SPIKES or AFCE, and it is not an official or validated ACE-CT model.

Repository references: SPIKES protocol and AFCE framework.

Extensibility model

Generate, interpret, and measure are intentionally separate responsibilities

Patient-model plugin

Generates behavior

Async generate_response(state, clinician_input) produces a virtual-patient turn during the encounter.

→

Evaluator plugin

Interprets behavior

Async evaluate(db, session_id) interprets a completed session and returns the feedback contract.

→

Metrics plugin

Measures behavior

Sync compute(db, session_id) calculates observable research or analytics measurements.

Lifecycle: startup imports a fixed module list → modules register classes in the in-memory PluginRegistry → configuration/case choices resolve to registry keys → selections freeze on the session → core services invoke the selected interfaces.

Repository basis: backend/src/plugins/load_plugins.py, registry.py, the three interfaces/ protocols, and session/scoring/dialogue services. There is no filesystem plugin discovery.

Complementary lenses

Three views answer different questions about the same conversation

SPIKES

Question: Did the conversation follow a useful difficult-news structure?

Setting · Perception · Invitation · Knowledge · Emotion · Strategy/Summary

AFCE

Question: Where were emotional/appraisal opportunities, elicitations, responses, and misses?

Feeling · Judgment · Appreciation · discourse relations

ACE-CT-inspired

Question: What broader communication behaviors are visible—and which are not observable—in this transcript?

Respond · Listen · Speak · proposed General group

Compatibility is not equivalence. Projecting ACE-CT-inspired results into APEX empathy, communication, overall, and external SPIKES fields is provisional engineering compatibility—not an official ACE-CT mapping.

Experimental integration architecture

Recommendation

Build the smallest defensible comparison demo—and freeze it early

  1. Restore and verify the full application workflow.
    Case → conversation → feedback → admin review.
  2. Obtain ACE-CT scientific and disclosure decisions.
  3. Run minimal controlled Gemini / OpenAI smoke tests.
    Only after authorization; capture exact provenance.
  4. Implement a read-only transcript-plus-comparison UI.
  5. Add deterministic transcript / evidence metrics if time permits.
  6. Establish a small expert-reviewed evaluation set.
  7. Freeze prompts, rubric, provider/model, fixtures, and provenance.
  8. Prepare paper, architecture figure, demo video, and offline fallback.

Stretch only

Minimal adaptive-patient state

Attempt only after the core demonstration works end to end and remains reproducible.

Explicitly avoid before submission

Several large new plugins

Do not divide the remaining time across speech-to-speech, mental-health specialization, supervised ACE-CT models, or multiple new LLM evaluators.

Demo thesis: APEX is useful because it makes patient simulation, evaluator experimentation, evidence, provenance, and comparison inspectable—not because it maximizes plugin count.
