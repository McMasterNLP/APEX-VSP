Inspiration—not implementation commitments

Ten public sources suggest several coherent expansion directions

Adaptive-VP

Lee et al. (2025)

Virtual-patient behavior adapts to trainee dialogue. Could inspire an explicit adaptive state in APEX.

Clinician behavior→trust · distress · understanding · disclosure→adapt response

SFMSS

Bao et al. (2025)

Medical simulation generation is guided by encounter/service flow. Could inspire phase-aware patient generation.

Encounter phase→permitted info + state→phase-aware response

PATIENT-ψ

Wang et al. (2024)

LLM virtual patients support structured mental-health training. Could inspire domain-bounded state and disclosure policies.

Mental-health case→state + disclosure policy→rapport-sensitive response

Emotion-aware task dialogue

Feng et al. (2024)

Emotion understanding, management, and generation are modeled across the dialogue loop. Could inspire explicit affect transitions.

Clinician turn→emotion-state update→strategy → language

Empathy intent

Jiang et al. (2023)

Empathy detection benefits from modeling communicative intent. Could inspire an intent-oriented evaluator beside observable AFCE signals.

Clinician response→intent classification→target + appropriateness + evidence

Non-emotion-centric empathy

Huangfu et al. (2025)

Empathic generation can draw on context and commonsense beyond predicted emotion labels. Could inspire broader cognitive/supportive behavior analysis.

Conversation→cognitive + supportive behavior→broader empathy analysis

Speech-to-speech VSP

Emerson et al. (2026)

Realtime speech interaction enables more natural virtual-patient encounters. Could inspire an audio-capable patient path and timing metrics.

Clinician speech→realtime dialogue→patient speech + audio metrics

Medical dialogue survey

Shi et al. (2024)

The survey organizes medical dialogue systems, evaluation, and challenges. Could inspire a multi-criterion simulation-quality report.

Patient responses→consistency · relevance · safety · realism→quality report

Lahnala et al. 2022

Critical Reflection on Empathy and NLP

Empathy NLP needs clear constructs, cognitive-empathy attention, and stronger construct validity. Could guide theory-to-measure alignment.

Theory→explicit construct→aligned detector / evaluator → validity

Lahnala et al. 2024

Appraisal Framework for Clinical Empathy

AFCE and SPIKES support discourse-level analysis of empathy relations in breaking-bad-news conversations. Could deepen evidence-linked communication feedback.

Patient / clinician spans→relations + SPIKES→metrics + feedback

All directions are inspired by the linked public works. They do not claim reproduction of any system, method, dataset, findings, or model.
